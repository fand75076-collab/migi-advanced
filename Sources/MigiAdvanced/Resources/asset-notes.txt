Migi Advanced v0.10 uses an 8 x 21 full-character atlas. Rows 13-20 restore
Migi's right-hand symbiont topology with normal-hand, palm-observer,
signal-sampling, calm-diagnosis, storage-pocket, recovery-care,
detached-scout-return, complete-sleep, and protective-shield families. The
legacy three-legged rows remain available only for short detached scouting and
anime-modernized device interactions. Every family uses authored full poses,
per-frame timing, eye anchors, tissue zones and rigid prop zones.

migi-parts.png (and its unused parts-based renderer MigiMetalView.swift) was
removed from the bundle in v0.14.1; the source material stays in
Assets/migi-parts-source.png. The active renderer never assembles Migi from
detachable template parts.
